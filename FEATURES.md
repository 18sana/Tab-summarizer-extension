# 🚀 Chrome Tab Summarizer — Features & Demo Guide

This guide outlines every user-facing feature of the **Chrome Tab Summarizer** extension. Use this document as your roadmap for the demo to showcase the product's value, features, and capabilities clearly.

---

## 🌟 Core Product Features

### 1. Context-Aware Highlight & Note Capture
* **How it works:** Whenever you are reading an article, documentation, or code, you can select any text block, right-click, and select **"Add to Tab Archive Note"**.
* **Immediate Feedback:** A sleek confirmation toast notification slides in at the corner of your screen to confirm the note is captured safely.
* **Multi-Snippet Collection:** You can capture multiple distinct sections on a single webpage. The extension automatically compiles them under the corresponding tab.

### 2. Interactive Workspace Preview Desk
* **Dynamic Popup Dashboard:** Clicking the extension icon in your Chrome bar opens a clean, side-by-side dashboard of all your active open tabs and captured notes.
* **Live Review & Editing:** You can review your gathered highlights, type custom summaries or comments, remove unwanted snippets, and double-check your workspace before saving.
* **Selective Archival:** Choose exactly which tabs to archive and keep open, keeping you in full control of your browser space.

### 3. Smart De-duplication & Page Appending
* **Zero Database Noise:** If you save a webpage that you have already archived in the past, the extension prevents duplicate entries from polluting your database.
* **Automatic Merging:** The extension detects the existing Notion page and cleanly appends your new highlights, comments, and timestamps directly to the bottom of the card, consolidating your research into a single source of truth.

### 4. Dedicated Archive History
* **Chronological Timeline:** A beautiful dashboard that keeps a history of every tab you have ever archived.
* **Quick Access:** Instantly re-open previously saved tabs, browse past notes, and review your compiled research timeline from a centralized interface.

### 5. Instant Workspace Search
* **Unified Search Bar:** A dedicated search engine built right into the extension.
* **Deep Filtering:** Search through your active open tabs, captured snippets, and archived history simultaneously to locate any bookmark or note in seconds.

### 6. Domain-Based Analytics & Productivity Metrics
* **Automatic Classification:** The extension automatically categorizes your archived pages based on their domain (e.g., Development, News, Shopping, Research).
* **Visual Insights:** A dashboard featuring interactive charts that show your reading habits, domain distributions, and time saved by clearing workspace clutter.

## 🎬 Step-by-Step Demo Flow

Follow this sequence during your presentation to demonstrate the full feature set in under 2 minutes:

### **Step 1: Highlight and Capture (Show the context flow)**
1. Open any blog or documentation page.
2. Select an important sentence or code block, right-click, and choose **"Add to Tab Archive Note"**.
3. Point out the toast notification confirming the capture. Highlight another sentence and repeat to show multi-snippet grouping.

### **Step 2: Review in the Workspace Desk (Show the popup flow)**
1. Click the extension icon in your Chrome bar.
2. Show the audience the **Workspace Preview Desk** containing all open tabs and your newly captured highlights.
3. Make a quick text edit in the popup notes to demonstrate real-time customization.

### **Step 3: Show Intelligent Appending (Show the database de-duplication)**
1. Re-open a page that was previously archived.
2. Highlight a new line of text, right-click, and capture it.
3. Open your Notion database and show that **no new card was created**. Instead, the existing page was updated, and the new highlight was appended cleanly to the bottom.

### **Step 4: History, Search, and Analytics (Show the dashboards)**
1. Click the **History** tab to show the timeline of your research.
2. Type a query in the **Search** field to show how instantly it finds matching text inside both open and archived tabs.
3. Click on the **Analytics** page to show the category charts representing domain distribution and productivity savings.
